MetroGnome

Metronome designed for teaching children. MetroGnome helps kids discover the perfect tempo from a wide selection, fine-tune beats per minute and bars. Visual and audio cues guide them effortlessly—the dwarf's eyes pulse in perfect sync, a bee lands on his nose at the bar's end, and a series of bees tracks each beat, with the open-winged one marking the moment.

Engaging Visual Cues

The dwarf's eyes move precisely with every tick. Bees fly in formation overhead, representing beats and subdivisions. It's intuitive, captivating, and keeps young musicians focused without distraction.

Flexible Audio Control

Clear, precise sounds mark tempo and bars. Toggle audio independently—click the earplug button to mute, and the dwarf closes his ears. Pair it with visuals or use standalone for silent practice.

Separate Visual and Audio Modes

Customize the experience: stop animations with an eye button (dwarf closes eyes), mute sound separately. Children adapt it to their needs, building rhythm skills their way.

Proven with Kids

Tested extensively—children love practicing with MetroGnome. It turns metronome time into play, fostering steady timing and musical confidence effortlessly.

How It Works

1. Select Tempo

Choose from a broad range of tempos or dial in beats per minute and bars precisely.

2. Start the Beat

Visuals and audio sync instantly—eyes pulse, bees fly, sounds click in rhythm.

3. Customize Modes

Toggle visuals (eye button) or audio (earplugs) independently for your preferred setup.

4. Practice Freely

Adjust on the fly. The dwarf's responsive animations and cues keep kids engaged bar after bar.
